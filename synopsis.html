Synopsis

1971. The Warrens are called to a farmhouse where a family has started sleeping with the lights on. Cold spots move room to room. Furniture turns over in empty houses. Something knows every name in the family.

Lorraine feels it before she sees it — a presence tied to Bathsheba, a woman hanged more than a century before, who gave her firstborn to the dark and has been offering other people's children ever since.

Behind a bricked-over closet wall, Ed finds a crawl-space no one built into the original house. The walls are covered floor to ceiling with a single scrawled line. What happens next is the reason the Warrens stopped taking cases without a priest on call.
