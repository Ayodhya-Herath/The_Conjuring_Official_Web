Credits & Behind the Scenes

In-World Cast

  • Ed Warren — investigator, demonologist
  • Lorraine Warren — clairvoyant, light-trance medium
  • Bathsheba — the accused witch, the presence in the house
  • The family — residents of the farmhouse

AI Tools & Models Used

  • ChatGPT — mood board reference imagery, color palette generation, and color-meaning writeups
  • Google Flow / Nano Banana 2 — character photography and video generation for Ed and Lorraine (full-body, close-up, front/side/back turntable)
  • Gemini — production design imagery for the hidden room (front, back, side, and floor-plan views)

How This Was Made

Every visual asset in this production — storyboard, mood board, characters, production design, poster, and scene — was generated through layered AI prompts, refined across iterations to keep character identity, costume, lighting, and color palette consistent across every shot. Human editing was limited to arranging shots, subtitles, sound mixing, color correction, and export.
